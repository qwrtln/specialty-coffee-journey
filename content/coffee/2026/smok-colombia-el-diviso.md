This one was considerably (~60%) more expensive than the [predecessor](/coffee/2026/smok-tanzania-mondul-estate/), and the prominent mention of "funky" in blue on the bag does this coffee justice.
It was a true fruity-floral bomb.
Probably this is the key characteristic of the [thermal shock](/tags/thermal-shock) processing, which so far I've encountered only on coffees from Colombia.
Drinking this coffee felt almost like eating a bag of sweet-sour gummy bears, with an additional strong and pleasant floral scent.
