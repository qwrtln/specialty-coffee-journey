I find it incredible that even on an island with not even a million residents (but probably a lot more tourists) there's enough market demand that warrants a specialty coffee roastery.
In my brew, "naranja" was hard to find, but the umbrella term "frutos rojos" actually captured the taste profile well, as raspberries and redcurrant were noticeable.
